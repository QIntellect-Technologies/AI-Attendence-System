┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ ⚙️ HOW IT WORKS (TECHNICAL FLOW)                                           │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

USER CLICKS "Start Camera"
         ↓
BROWSER REQUESTS CAMERA PERMISSION
         ↓
USER CLICKS "Allow"
         ↓
CAMERA STREAM STARTS
         ↓
FRAMES CAPTURED EVERY 500ms (2 FPS)
         ↓
FRAME SENT TO SERVER (/api/recognize/frame)
         ↓
YOLOV8 DETECTS FACES
         ↓
INSIGHTFACE EXTRACTS FACE EMBEDDINGS
         ↓
COMPARE WITH ENROLLED PROFILES (Imran, Hooria)
         ↓
COSINE SIMILARITY MATCHING
         ↓
RESULT: Name + Confidence Score
         ↓
UPDATE UI IN REAL-TIME
         ↓
STATISTICS COUNTER INCREMENTS

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🐛 TROUBLESHOOTING                                                         │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

PROBLEM: Camera won't start
SOLUTION:
  1. Allow browser permission when prompted
  2. Check if camera is already in use
  3. Try different browser
  4. Restart browser

PROBLEM: No faces detected
SOLUTION:
  1. Improve lighting (natural light preferred)
  2. Move closer to camera (30-60cm)
  3. Ensure face is clearly visible
  4. Remove glasses/masks if possible

PROBLEM: Wrong person detected
SOLUTION:
  1. Re-enroll person with better video
  2. Ensure good lighting during enrollment
  3. Check face quality during enrollment
  4. Increase enrollment video duration

PROBLEM: Low confidence scores
SOLUTION:
  1. Improve lighting conditions
  2. Move closer to camera
  3. Ensure face is clearly visible
  4. Re-enroll with better quality video

┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ 🎓 TIPS FOR BEST RESULTS                                                   │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

✓ Good Lighting
  └─ Natural light or bright room
  └─ Avoid backlighting
  └─ Ensure face is well-lit

✓ Clear Face
  └─ No glasses or sunglasses
  └─ No masks or obstructions
  └─ Face fully visible

✓ Close Distance
  └─ 30-60cm from camera
  └─ Not too close, not too far
  └─ Optimal for face detection

✓ Centered Position
  └─ Face in middle of frame
  └─ Head straight (not tilted)
  └─ Eyes looking at camera

✓ Still Position
  └─ Minimize head movement
  └─ Keep face steady
  └─ Avoid rapid movements
