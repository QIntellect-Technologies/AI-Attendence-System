PROBLEM IDENTIFIED:
- Bounding box flickers between "Imran Khalid" and "Unknown"
- Happens because tracking distance threshold (120px) is too small for NVR 
  cameras at 6-8 feet distance
- When person moves >120px between frames, system creates new track
- New track shows "Unknown" until analyzed
- This causes flickering effect

ROOT CAUSE:
At 6-8 feet distance, a person's head can move 150-200 pixels between frames
due to:
- Normal head movement
- Camera angle changes
- Body movement
- Frame-to-frame variation

Old threshold (120px) was too small for this distance

   Location: Line ~786
