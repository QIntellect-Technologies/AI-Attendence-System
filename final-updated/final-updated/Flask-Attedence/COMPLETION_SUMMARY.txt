================================================================================

QUICK START (3 Steps):

1. CREATE PROFILE
   - Go to "👤 Staff Enrollment" tab
   - Enter full name (e.g., "Imran Khalid")
   - Enter email (e.g., "imran@example.com")

   - Click "Record Video (20s)" button

   - Embeddings stored in database
   - Ready for live detection
